# Scientific Verification & NASA Source Reference Layer

This directory serves as the scientific authority for the **Stellar Stories** interactive children's storybook. All scientific statements, visual representations, and narrative claims must be verified against official NASA sources before implementation in the story engine.

---

## Non-Negotiable Scientific Principles

1. **Distinguish Normal Solar Wind vs. Extreme Solar Events**
   - *Normal Solar Wind*: Continuous stream of charged particles (protons and electrons) originating from the Sun's corona, responsible for regular polar auroras.
   - *Coronal Mass Ejections (CMEs)*: Massive bursts of solar wind and magnetic fields released into space during high solar activity. CMEs can cause significant geomagnetic storms when directed toward Earth.
   - *Rule*: Never state or imply that "every aurora is caused by a CME."

2. **Probabilistic Language for Technology Impacts**
   - Space weather can affect satellite operations, GPS/GNSS signal propagation, high-frequency radio communications, and ground electric power grids.
   - *Rule*: Never present technology impacts as guaranteed disasters or total apocalyptic blackouts. Use clear, child-friendly probabilistic phrasing:
     - *"Strong space-weather events can affect navigation signals..."*
     - *"Radio communications can sometimes be disrupted..."*
     - *"Electric power grids require careful monitoring during geomagnetic storms..."*

3. **Narrative Scope Constraint**
   - Lina remains the sole protagonist. Technology impacts are shown strictly through her immediate world (her navigation equipment, radio static, forecast app).
